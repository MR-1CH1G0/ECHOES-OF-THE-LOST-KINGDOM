Introduction — The World Before the Darkness

Long ago, the Heart of Eternity protected Eldoria. When King Aldric shattered it to contain an ancient being called the Hollow One, darkness swallowed the kingdom. The king vanished, and the truth was buried.

Fifteen years later, shadow creatures attack the village where Kael has grown up. A mysterious guardian gives him a broken sword and one warning: find the fragments, uncover the truth, and never trust the king.

Now Kael must cross eight dangerous regions to decide the fate of Eldoria.

Prototype controls: use the left joystick to move. Tap JUMP, SWORD, or FIRE. This starter version uses simple generated 3D shapes; later, you can replace them with custom models, animations, sound, and detailed terrain.
